01 / OVERVIEW

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how FlashDrop handles information when you use the FlashDrop Android application (“FlashDrop”, “the app”, “we”, “our” or “us”). FlashDrop is a nearby file-sharing application that uses Wi-Fi Direct to transfer content between devices.

In the current version, FlashDrop does not require an account and does not use an internet-based developer server to receive or store your transfers. Transfers are initiated by users and are sent directly between the participating devices over a local connection.

02 / INFORMATION & PERMISSIONS

Information and permissions

Files and content you choose

When you choose to send a file, photo, video, audio file, document or other supported content, FlashDrop accesses the selected item so it can transfer it to the device you choose. The app does not upload these items to FlashDrop-operated servers.

Location permission

Android may require location permission for Wi-Fi Direct discovery on some Android versions. FlashDrop uses the relevant permission for nearby-device discovery. The current app is not designed to track your physical movements, build a location history, or send your GPS location to FlashDrop servers.

Installed apps

If you use the feature to share an installed app, FlashDrop may inspect the apps available on your device so you can select an app package to share. This information is used locally for that feature and is not sent to a FlashDrop server.

Messages and other content

Content you type or select for transfer is transmitted to the nearby device you connect to. Only send content to devices and people you trust.

03 / DEVICE-TO-DEVICE TRANSFERS

How transfers work

FlashDrop is designed to transfer content directly between nearby devices using Wi-Fi Direct. The receiving device may save a copy of the content it receives. Once a transfer reaches another device, that copy is under the control of the recipient.

Transfers are started by the user. Make sure the selected receiving device is the intended recipient before sending files or messages. FlashDrop does not control how a recipient uses, stores, copies or deletes content after receiving it.

The current version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send usage analytics to an advertising or analytics provider, based on the app configuration described for this release.

04 / SECURITY

Security considerations

We take privacy seriously, but no method of electronic communication or storage is completely risk-free. The current version does not provide app-level end-to-end encryption for Wi-Fi Direct transfers. Do not transfer sensitive or confidential information unless you understand and accept this limitation.

Use FlashDrop with devices you recognise and trust, and keep your device operating system up to date.

05 / STORAGE & DELETION

Storage and deletion

FlashDrop does not maintain a cloud account or a developer-operated server containing your transferred files. Files received through FlashDrop may remain in the storage location used by your device or the app until you delete them.

To remove a received file, use your device's file manager or the app's available delete controls, if provided. You may also clear app data or uninstall FlashDrop; this may remove app-local information, but it does not delete copies already saved on another person's device.

06 / SHARING & THIRD PARTIES

Sharing and third parties

FlashDrop does not sell your personal information. The app does not send your transferred content to advertising networks, analytics providers or other third-party online services in the current release.

When you deliberately send content to another device, that device and its user receive the content you selected. This is the core file-sharing function of FlashDrop.

07 / CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Children's privacy

FlashDrop is a general-purpose file-sharing utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us. Because the current app does not provide an online account or developer-operated data collection service, we may have limited ability to identify or remove information stored only on a user's device.

08 / POLICY CHANGES

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, permissions or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Please review this page periodically.
